# Golbal-AI-Adoption-Trends-and-Data-Analysis

# AI Company Adoption EDA

## Overview  
This project presents an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of AI adoption across companies worldwide.
Using a dataset of 150,000 rows × 43 columns, it examines company demographics, AI adoption metrics, business outcomes, 
and governance indicators. The analysis highlights adoption rates, productivity impacts, industry trends, and employee satisfaction, providing actionable insights into how AI is transforming organizations globally.

## Tools & Technologies  
- Python – Core programming language  
- Pandas & NumPy – Data manipulation and statistical analysis  
- Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization  
- Jupyter Notebook – Interactive environment for analysis  

## Key Findings  
- AI Adoption Rate: Average of 36.41% across companies  
- Productivity Change: Average increase of +9.27%  
- Remote Work Impact: Productivity rises from ~6% (0–25% remote) to ~14.5% (76–100% remote)  
- Company Age Effect: Mature firms (30+ years) outperform younger startups in productivity gains  
- Industry Insights: Technology sector dominates (21,107 entries), with Software Development and Marketing Automation driving the highest gains  
- Adoption Stages: Majority in partial adoption (~78,800 responses); pilot experimentation still common (~5,198 responses)  
- Employee Satisfaction: Higher in companies with full AI adoption compared to those with none
